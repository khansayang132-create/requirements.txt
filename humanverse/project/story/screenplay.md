# The Kite That Came Home

*When the wind snaps the string of her late grandmother's kite, nine-year-old Amara learns that a neighbourhood is a kind of family.*

Audience: families, ages 5-10 · Genre: family comedy-drama · Theme: small kindnesses find their way back

## S01 EXT. FERNMOOR PARK - DAY

_Purpose: Set up the bond between Amara and Grandpa and the heirloom kite._

**CHAR-HUM-001** (excited): Grandpa! The wind is perfect today!  
**CHAR-HUM-004** (happy): Then let's wake up Grandma's old dragon kite.  
**CHAR-HUM-001** (laughing): Look how high it goes!  

## S02 EXT. FERNMOOR PARK - MOMENTS LATER

_Purpose: The loss: the string breaks; her brother turns tears into a plan._

**CHAR-HUM-001** (crying): No... the string broke. It's gone!  
**CHAR-HUM-002** (worried): Hey, don't cry. Kites can't fly far without wind.  
**CHAR-HUM-002** (happy): Come on. We'll find it together.  

## S03 EXT. MAPLE STREET - SUNSET

_Purpose: The return: the neighbour found it; kindness comes back and the circle grows._

**CHAR-HUM-005** (happy): Is this little dragon looking for someone?  
**CHAR-HUM-001** (surprised): Our kite! You found it!  
**CHAR-HUM-005** (happy): It landed in my lemon tree.
